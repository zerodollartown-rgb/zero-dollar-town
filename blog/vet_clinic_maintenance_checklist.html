Vet Clinic Maintenance Checklist

Ensure your veterinary practice avoids data gaps that lead to lost appointments, confused clients, and revenue leaks.

General Data Hygiene Checks (All Industries)

  • Sign #1: Check if email open rates drop mid-funnel despite high ad spend.
  • Sign #2: Verify if lead status stalling in CRM vs. Website (e.g., "Interested" never becomes "Lead").
  • Sign #3: Scan support tickets for phrases like "Wrong Info," "Old Price," or duplicate inquiries from same person.

Vet Specific Checks

  1. Appointment Sync: When a client books an appointment on our website, is that date visible to the vet scheduling software?
  2. Vaccination Tracking: If we collect vaccination info online via Google Forms, does it populate into the practice management system automatically?
  3. Pricing Accuracy: Do nutrition plan PDFs sent after consultations reference prices or packages that changed two months ago?
