Industry Pain Point Audit Checklist

Are Your Process Gaps Costing You $3.3M+?
Use this checklist to identify operational risks in HVAC, Dental, or Veterinary practices before they become revenue leaks.

Pillar 1: Data Integrity & Logging

✅ Do you track every service call?

The Risk: Without full audit trails, billing disputes and warranty claims go unresolved.

Audit Question: Can I pull a complete log of who visited the site in real-time if needed? If no, you are vulnerable to fraud or compliance gaps (like Ransomware).

✅ Are customer notes stored securely?

The Risk: Unstructured data leads to lost opportunities and privacy breaches.

Action: Ensure all client interactions are captured in a unified, secure database before the end of your shift.

Pillar 2: Lead Qualification & Conversion

✅ Do you know where leads originate?

The Risk: Losing visibility into traffic sources means losing budget efficiency.

Audit Question: When a lead comes in, can I instantly see which source (organic search, referral, ad) generated them? If not, you are leaking marketing spend.

✅ Is your follow-up automated?

The Risk: Manual delays cause leads to go cold before conversion.

Action: Implement a checklist that triggers immediately upon lead capture. If the system does not log this, it is an operational gap you must fix now.

Pillar 3: Compliance & Risk Mitigation

✅ Are your service agreements digitally signed and stored?

The Risk: Verbal contracts are unenforceable in modern disputes.

Check: Review the last 50 files. If any lack a digital timestamp or secure storage path, you have an immediate liability risk.

✅ Do your staff use standardized SOPs?

The Risk: Inconsistent service quality damages brand reputation and leads to lawsuits.
