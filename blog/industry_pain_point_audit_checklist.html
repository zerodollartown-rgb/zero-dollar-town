HVAC Industry

Data Silo Risk (HVAC)

High Impact ($4.8M Annual Cost Avoidance)

The Problem: Inconsistent data tracking across service calls leads to massive revenue leakage and compliance gaps.

✓ Implement Centralized Log

Consolidate all service call data into a single database to prevent silos.

✓ Audit Customer Records

Run an immediate audit of customer records for missing metadata tags.

Dentistry Industry

Data Silo Risk (Dental)

High Impact ($150k Exposure Risk)

The Problem: Failure to properly track patient financial data creates significant write-off risks.

✓ Verify Payment Gateways

Ensure all payment processing endpoints are secure and compliant (PCI-DSS).

✓ Update Billing Software

Synchronize billing software with insurance clearinghouse feeds.

Veterinary Industry

Data Silo Risk (Vet)

High Impact ($120k Write-off Risk)

The Problem: Fragmented inventory and clinical data leads to stockouts and medical errors.

✓ Sync Inventory Systems

Integrate pharmacy inventory with patient history for automated refills.

✓ Standardize Clinical Notes

Migrate handwritten notes to digital, structured formats immediately.
