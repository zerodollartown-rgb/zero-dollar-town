🔒 Data Independence Checklist for Your Business

Your business owns its data—if not, you're vulnerable.

  • ✓You can export all customer data in CSV/JSON format.
  • xYour vendor lets you download historical logs freely.
  • ✗You signed away data rights in a contract (red flag!)

This checklist helps you audit whether your company truly owns its data—or if it's trapped inside proprietary systems.
