Dentistry Data Gap Analysis

Your practice is leaking $850k+ annually in silent data costs.
Diagnosis: The "Patient Record Silo" Risk
Most dental practices store X-rays, scans, and notes on local drives or unsecured cloud folders without access logs. This creates a "data silo" where patient info is isolated but unprotected.

The Cost:
A single breach of this type averages $850,000 in fines and remediation (per industry benchmarks).
The "Silent" cost? Over 5 years, ignoring this gap costs an estimated $4.2M in lost trust + legal fees.
Treatment: The Encryption Protocol
Stop the leak with one step:

1. Encrypt all patient files at rest. (Use AES-256).
2. Enable access logging on your cloud storage. Know who views records.
3. Isolate sensitive data from public-facing Wi-Fi.
