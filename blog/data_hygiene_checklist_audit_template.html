Industry Pain Point Audit Checklist

Use this checklist to audit your business for the three critical signs of data silos that cost $10k+ monthly in lost revenue.

General Data Hygiene Checks (All Industries)

  • Sign #1: Check if email open rates drop mid-funnel despite high ad spend.
  • Sign #2: Verify if lead status stalling in CRM vs. Website (e.g., "Interested" never becomes "Lead").
  • Sign #3: Scan support tickets for phrases like "Wrong Info," "Old Price," or duplicate inquiries from same person.

HVAC Specific Checks

  • Are quote requests in the email tool not updating to "Qualified" status in Salesforce/HubSpot?
  • Do technician scheduling apps show different availability than what is promised on our landing pages?
  • If a customer calls asking about an old promo code, does the CRM know that offer expired last month?

Dentist Specific Checks

  • When a patient fills out an online form for whitening, does the front desk software get that lead instantly?
  • If we run Facebook ads targeting "braces," do those leads appear in our booking calendar or only in email logs?
  • Are hygiene reminder emails sent based on dates in a spreadsheet while appointments are booked directly in another system?

Vet Specific Checks

  • When a client books an appointment on our website, is that date visible to the vet scheduling software?
  • If we collect vaccination info online via Google Forms, does it populate into the practice management system automatically?
  • Do nutrition plan PDFs sent after consultations reference prices or packages that changed two months ago?

Action:

  1. Map Flows: Draw a simple line from Lead Capture -> CRM Status Change -> Notification Email.
  2. Test Data: Send yourself two identical leads through different paths (Web form vs. Phone call) and see where they diverge in your systems.
  3. Clean Olds: Archive or delete records older than 12 months that have no activity to reduce noise.
