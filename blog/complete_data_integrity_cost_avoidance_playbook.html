The average cost of a data breach is $4.8 million. For healthcare (Dental, Vet), it can exceed $30M+ due to HIPAA penalties and lost trust.

This playbook combines our industry-specific audits with actionable steps to avoid these costs.

HVAC Industry: Data Silos & Integrity Gaps

h3>Pain Point #1: Unintended Cross-System Leaks
When HVAC systems integrate with billing or scheduling platforms, customer PII can inadvertently sync without proper encryption or access controls.
Risk: If a third-party vendor (e.g., cloud storage) is compromised, your business inherits their breach liability even if you didn't store the data directly.

Dental Industry: PHI Exposure & Compliance Burdens

h3>Pain Point #1: Legacy Paper Records Digitized Without Security
Many dental practices still use paper charts or outdated digital systems that lack modern encryption. When these are migrated to cloud storage without proper protocols, patient data becomes vulnerable.
Risk: HIPAA penalties alone can exceed $20k per incident; combined with legal fees and reputation loss, total cost often hits millions.

Veterinary Industry: Sensitive Pet Owner Data & Insurance Claims

h3>Pain Point #1: Poorly Secured Client Intake Forms
Veterinary clinics often collect detailed health histories and owner contact info during intake. If these forms are stored locally or shared via email without encryption, they become easy targets.
Risk: A single breach can expose sensitive pet medical records (similar to PHI), triggering state-level privacy laws beyond HIPAA.
