Your 5-Point Data Hygiene Checklist

Follow these steps to clear the "blind spots" in your dental business operations.

  • 1. Verify Patient Profile Completeness:
    Check that every appointment is linked to a full patient profile (including past treatment history). If this link is broken, you are not upselling care plans effectively.
  • 2. Unify Lead Sources:
    Ensure leads from Google Ads and your website form land in the same database as clinical staff notes.
    If they don't connect automatically, you are losing high-value treatment estimates.
  • 3. Standardize Terminology:
    Map internal acronyms (e.g., "Crown", "Implant") to standardized tags in your practice management software.
    Inconsistent tagging prevents accurate reporting on seasonal demand and inventory needs.
  • 4. Automate Follow-Up Triggers:
    Set up rules that automatically send a recall notice or hygiene reminder after 6 months of activity.
    If this automation is missing, you are manually chasing revenue instead of letting the system work.
  • 5. Audit Third-Party Integrations:
    Review which tools (e.g., billing software) feed data into your main practice management tool.
    Ensure they export in a format that is easy to import back, preventing "data rot" over time.
