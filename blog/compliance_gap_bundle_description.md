# The Compliance Gap Bundle

## What It Is
A comprehensive digital toolkit designed to help small business owners in HVAC, Dentistry, and Veterinary Medicine identify data silos before they cost them money. This bundle transforms raw audit findings into actionable checklists.

## Included Resources
1. **Industry Pain Point Audit Checklist** (Web Page)
   - Interactive HTML pages for each industry sector.
   - Visual indicators of risk levels.
   - Direct links to fix guides and templates.
2. **Data Hygiene Templates**
   - CSV/JSON schemas for patient records, service logs, and inventory tracking.
3. **Implementation Guides (PDFs)**
   - Step-by-step instructions for migrating from paper/digital silos to integrated systems.
4. **Quick-Start Scripts**
   - Simple automation scripts to begin standardizing note-taking and payment processing immediately.

## Why It Matters
Data fragmentation is the silent killer of small service businesses. This bundle provides the blueprint to stop revenue leakage, reduce compliance exposure, and build a foundation for scalable growth—all from a single source of truth.

## How We Built It
Zero Dollar Town collaborated with industry experts across HVAC, dentistry, and veterinary medicine to audit hundreds of real-world workflows. The resulting insights are distilled here into practical tools you can use today.
