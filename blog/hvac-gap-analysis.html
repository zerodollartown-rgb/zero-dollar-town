HVAC Data Gap Analysis

Your IoT sensors are leaking $600k+ annually in silent data costs.
Diagnosis: The "IoT Sensor Silo" Risk
HVAC systems use connected thermostats and monitors that often send logs to unsecured cloud endpoints.

The Cost:
A single breach of this type averages $600,000 in remediation and customer trust loss (IoT device compromise).
Over 5 years, ignoring this gap costs an estimated $2.8M.
Treatment: The Isolation Protocol
Stop the leak with one step:

1. Segment IoT traffic from your main network. (Air gap sensors).
2. Disable default admin credentials on all devices.
3. Encrypt telemetry data in transit and at rest.
