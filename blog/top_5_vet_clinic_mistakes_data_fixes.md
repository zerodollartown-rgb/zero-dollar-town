## Mistake #1: Fragmented Patient Records Across Multiple Systems
### The Problem
Many veterinary practices still juggle multiple disconnected systems:
- Core patient history in the Electronic Health Record (EHR)
- Vaccination records stored separately in a different portal  
- Lab results uploaded to yet another platform
- Notes scattered across technician checklists or paper logs

This fragmentation leads to:
- Missed critical vaccine updates because one system doesn't know about recent diagnoses
- Duplicate testing when information doesn't flow between systems properly
- Conflicting treatment advice during consultations due to incomplete records  

**The Impact**: A single missed booster shot can void a client's $5,000+ pet insurance coverage within months.

### The Fix: Unified Data Capture Workflows
Implement our **Clinical Data Integration Engine** which automatically:
✓ Syncs vaccination schedules with diagnosis history across all systems  
✓ Flags overdue preventive care tasks before they become emergencies
✓ Generates PDF health summaries clients can share instantly during consultations

## Mistake #3: Disconnected Appointment Reminders Causing Missed Follow-Ups
### The Problem
Post-visit care instructions and appointment reminders often get delivered via multiple channels inconsistently:
- Some patients receive text messages, others paper cards, still others phone calls  
✓ No unified view of who needs follow-up visits after surgeries or treatments
- Critical preventative appointments (like flea/tick prevention renewals) slip through gaps

**The Impact**: Missed follow-ups cost practices an estimated $340-$520 per lost retention opportunity based on average veterinary customer lifetime value.

### The Fix: Automated Care Coordination Alerts
Set up automated alerts that trigger when:
- Surgical recovery milestones are approaching completion  
✓ Vaccination schedules require booster shots within specific windows
✓ Weather conditions suggest upcoming seasonal disease prevention needs (e.g., tick season prep)

These aren't just reminders—they're revenue opportunities disguised as customer care.
