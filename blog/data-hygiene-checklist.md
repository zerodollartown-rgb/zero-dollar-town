# Small Business Data Hygiene Checklist & Audit Template

## Why This Matters: The Cost of Silence

Every day your business data sits in disconnected silos, you risk losing **$3.31M** (the average cost of a mid-sized breach) or worse.

This checklist isn't just about cleaning up spreadsheets; it's an operational audit to prevent process gaps that lead to downtime and liability. Use this template to prove your "Operational Risk Assurance" capability before selling any premium package.

---

## Phase 1: Inventory & Visibility (The Foundation)

**Goal:** Know exactly what data lives where, so you can automate it or delete it safely.

- [ ] **Asset Map Complete?** List every digital folder, cloud drive link, and software account used for customer/financial data. *Action:* Create a master spreadsheet listing Owner, Location (Local vs Cloud), Access Level, and Last Audit Date.
- [ ] **Orphaned Data Identified?** Scan your file system for files older than 12 months that have not been accessed since the last calendar year. *Risk:* These are high-value targets for attackers or compliance violations.
- [ ] **Redundancy Check:** Do you have a documented backup plan? If one drive fails, can you recover customer PII within 4 hours?

---

## Phase 2: Security Hygiene (The Lock)

**Goal:** Ensure your data isn't accessible to unauthorized parties.

- [ ] **Password Manager Audit:** Is every staff member using a password manager? Are all accounts protected by MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)? *Action:* Run a report on the admin dashboard for any shared logins or missing 2FA flags.
- [ ] **Access Levels Review:** Do employees have access to data they don't need today based on their current role?

---

## Phase 3: Process Automation (The Flow)

**Goal:** Stop manual work and prevent human error through automated workflows.

- [ ] **Duplicate Detection:** Are there duplicate customer records across your CRM, email marketing tools, and invoice software? *Action:* Run a deduplication query on the primary data source immediately.
- [ ] **Workflow Gaps Identified?** Map out critical business processes (e.g., "New Lead to Invoice"). Highlight every step that currently requires manual intervention or human judgment.

---
## Phase 4: Implementation Roadmap

1.  **Fix:** Address immediate high-risk items from Phases 2 & 3 first.
2.  **Automate:** Replace the identified workflow gaps with simple scripts, Zapier integrations, or API connections to reduce manual touchpoints by at least 50% within 90 days.
3.  **Verify:** Re-run Phase 1 after two weeks of new workflows to ensure data integrity holds up under load.
