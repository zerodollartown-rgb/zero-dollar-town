Your 5-Point Data Hygiene Checklist

Follow these steps to clear the "blind spots" in your HVAC business operations.

1. Verify Customer Profile Completeness:
Check that every job order is linked to a full customer profile (including past maintenance history). If this link is broken, you are not upselling service plans effectively.
2. Unify Lead Sources:
Ensure leads from Google Ads and your website form land in the same database as field technicians' notes.
If they don't connect automatically, you are losing high-value repair estimates.
3. Standardize Terminology:
Map internal acronyms (e.g., "AC", "Cooling") to standardized tags in your CRM.
Inconsistent tagging prevents accurate reporting on seasonal demand and inventory needs.
4. Automate Follow-Up Triggers:
Set up rules that automatically send a compliance check or maintenance reminder after 30 days of inactivity.
If this automation is missing, you are manually chasing revenue instead of letting the system work.
5. Audit Third-Party Integrations:
Review which tools (e.g., accounting software) feed data into your main CRM.
Ensure they export in a format that is easy to import back, preventing "data rot" over time.
