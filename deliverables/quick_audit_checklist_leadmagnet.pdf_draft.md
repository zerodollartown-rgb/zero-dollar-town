# Quick Operational Audit Checklist (Digital Lead Magnet)

**Goal:** Simple, low-friction download to capture leads immediately after the initial website visit.

**Structure:** 
1. **Pillar 1: Data Flow Mapping:** Do you know where every piece of customer data enters and exits your business? (Yes/No/Unsure)
2. **Pillar 2: Compliance Gap Check:** Which critical regulations (HIPAA, local zoning, etc.) have *not* been reviewed by a third party in the last 12 months? 
3. **Pillar 3: Process Bottleneck Identification:** List your top 3 manual processes that require more than one person's input. 

**CTA:** 'Send us your answers to this checklist, and we will provide a prioritized remediation plan—no commitment required.' (Feeds into Mila's marketing/lead capture flow.)
