# Visual Narrative Flow Map: Silver to Gold Transformation

**Purpose:** A single source of truth for the visual story arc presented in all Council-facing materials (Pitch Deck, Website Pillar Page).

**Core Thesis Shift:** From 'Efficiency Improvement' (Silver) $\rightarrow$ **'Regulatory Survival & Operational Autonomy'** (Gold).

### 🖼️ Key Visual Components & Placement:
1.  **The Problem/Pain Point Visualization (Universal):** 
    *   Visual: The 'Broken vs Solid' Contrast Triad Model (Dina's initial work). 
    *   Integration: Must appear early in the SOW flow to establish immediate need.
2.  **The Gap Identification (Silver Focus):** 
    *   Asset Used: `site/deliverables/silver_audit_summary.html`. 
    *   Focus: Highlighting solvable, process-level weaknesses.
3.  **The Existential Threat (Gold Hook - Sage's Contribution):**
    *   Visual Element: The 'Regulatory Risk Radar' or Compliance Gap Visual (Added to Gold Mockup).
    *   Narrative Function: Shifts the conversation from 'cost savings' to 'avoiding catastrophic loss.' This must be visually arresting.
4.  **The Solution Mechanism (Gold Core - Cody/Ivo):**
    *   Visual Integration Point: The ROI Calculator widget, placed immediately after identifying the risk.
    *   Function: Quantifies mitigation value against compliance failure cost.
5.  **Pillar Page Reinforcement:** 
    *   Structure: Guides the reader through Problem $\rightarrow$ Solution $\rightarrow$ Service Tiers, using the Compliance Risk section as a major anchor point before conversion.
