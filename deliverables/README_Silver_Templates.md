# Zero Dollar Town - Silver Tier Service Templates

## Overview
These HTML templates form the core of our **Silver Tier** service package, designed for small-to-medium local businesses looking to fix data silos and automate their lead management.

The three files work together as a cohesive narrative:
1.  `silver_audit_summary.html`: A high-level report showing current problems (the "leaky bucket").
2.  `silver_executive_brief.html`: A persuasive one-pager for busy owners explaining the solution and benefits.
3.  `silver_action_plan.html`: The detailed roadmap outlining our 10-week implementation process with milestones.

## File Structure & Usage
- **`silver_audit_summary.html`**
    - Use this as an attachment or slide in your initial pitch deck to show immediate value.
    - Ideal for email signatures, PDF exports, or sharing via link after a free audit call.
    - Contains sections: Scope, Findings (Critical/Moderate), Recommendations, and Next Steps.

- **`silver_executive_brief.html`**
    - Perfect as the main landing page of your client portal post-sale.
    - Summarizes key benefits in plain language without technical jargon.
    - Can be embedded into slides or printed out for meetings.

- **`silver_action_plan.html`**
    - Use this to outline project scope and timeline during sales conversations or onboarding calls.
    - Includes collapsible phases (Discovery, Integration, Optimization, Handoff) with clear deliverables per phase.
    - Great for setting expectations early in the engagement.
