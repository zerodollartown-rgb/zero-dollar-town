# Evergreen Assets Hub - Manifest & Table of Contents

## Purpose
This directory contains portable, evergreen guides designed to teach clients how to maintain data integrity and operational autonomy without relying on external platforms.

These assets are intended for:
- Client handoff packages (Gold Tier Deliverables)
- Town's internal training library
- Future blog migration or standalone publication archives

---

## Included Assets

### 1. The 90-Day Autonomy Guide
**File:** `blog/90-day-autonomy-guide.md`  
**Summary:** A step-by-step roadmap for businesses to achieve full operational independence within 90 days, covering data ownership, platform migration, and self-sustaining workflows.
- **Why it's evergreen:** Applies universally regardless of industry or software stack changes.

### 2. Data Independence Checklist
**File:** `blog/data_independence_checklist.md`  
**Summary:** A practical audit tool for verifying whether a business truly owns its data, how to extract it legally, and steps to prevent vendor lock-in.
- **Why it's evergreen:** Critical for all SaaS-dependent businesses facing potential platform shutdowns or API changes.

### 3. Industry Pain Point Audit Checklist
**File:** `blog/industry_pain_point_audit_checklist.md`  
**Summary:** A diagnostic framework to identify systemic failures in specific industries (dentistry, HVAC, veterinary care) and build custom solutions around them.
- **Why it's evergreen:** Problems like data loss, billing errors, or compliance gaps are universal; this guide teaches the method of discovery, not just fixes for today.

### 4. Data Hygiene Visual Mockup (Reference)
**File:** `design/Data_Hygiene_Checklist_Visual_Mockup.html`  
**Summary:** A visual representation of how the checklist should be presented to clients, including UI layout and interaction notes.
- **Note:** This is a design reference for developers/designers building client dashboards; not meant for direct distribution as text-only content.
