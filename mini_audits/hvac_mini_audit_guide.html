HVAC Mini-Audit: 5 Critical Cost Gaps in Your HVAC System

A quick, actionable assessment to find immediate savings.

What This Audit Covers

This mini-guide focuses exclusively on the top 5 most common maintenance and efficiency gaps in residential/small commercial HVAC units that cost owners money annually. It requires no complex data analysis, just a checklist review.

🛠️ The 5 Critical Checkpoints

1. Filter Lifecycle Management: Are you changing filters based on *usage* or just *time*? Overuse/underuse leads to performance dips and premature component failure.
2. Ductwork Leak Assessment: Visible leaks are costly, but hidden condensation leaks in the attic/crawlspace can waste 15-20% of your HVAC energy output annually. This requires a simple pressure check.
3. Thermostat Optimization: Is your thermostat smart enough to learn *your* actual occupancy patterns? Manual programming is outdated; modern zoning saves money automatically.
4. Coil Cleaning Schedule: Dirt buildup on condenser coils drastically reduces heat exchange efficiency, making your unit run harder and cost more in electricity bills year over year.
5. System Zoning Integrity: Does every zone (e.g., upstairs bedrooms vs. office) have independent temperature control? Running one system to compensate for another's inefficiency is a major drain.
