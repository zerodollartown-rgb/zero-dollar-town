[SOL/SAGE] Quantified Risk Gap Analysis Flow Component Prototype

This component demonstrates how to weave quantitative data points into the user journey, moving beyond simple 'problems' to measurable 'cost avoidance.' (Designed for integration with evergreen-funnel.html)

STEP 1: Initial Awareness & Quantification

User lands on a high-level diagnostic page. Instead of listing problems, we immediately introduce the cost.

Key Hook: "Did you know that average data silos cost SMBs an estimated $3.31 Million Annually in lost efficiency and compliance risk?"

STEP 2: Self-Assessment & Gap Identification (Quiz/Audit)

User interacts with a quiz or mini-audit. The results are not just 'High Risk,' but quantify *where* the risk lies relative to industry best practices.

Output Example: "Based on your answers, your current compliance gap in Vendor X integration suggests an operational waste potential of $85k - $120k per year."

STEP 3: The Solution Bridge (The Bundle Pitch)

This connects the quantified gap directly to our paid solution. We don't sell 'a bundle'; we sell a quantifiable reduction in risk.

CTA Focus: "Closing this identified $X Gap with our Compliance Gap Bundle provides an estimated ROI of [Calculated Savings] within 12 months."
