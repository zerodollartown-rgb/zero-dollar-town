## Quick Wins You Can Apply Today
1. Validate your intake funnel before investing in new tools.
2. Set up webhooks for instant lead delivery into your CRM.
3. Audit your digital plumbing: test every channel (Google, Facebook) to ensure data reaches dispatch/booking correctly.
4. Standardize fields like `CustomerName`, `ServiceType` across all touchpoints.
5. Use cloud-linked consent forms instead of local USB drives.
6. Sync time zones and billing codes automatically to avoid denials/no-shows.
7. Centralize VIP tracking into your main CRM, not spreadsheets.
