THE COMPLIANCE GAP BUNDLE AUDIT GUIDE

Executive Summary: The Cost of Complacency

**Are preventable data failures costing your revenue?** Across multiple high-value service sectors—Dentistry, HVAC, and Veterinary Medicine—we see a pattern: operational profit leaks through poor process integrity, manual workflows, and disconnected systems. This bundle exposes those hidden costs.

**Key Takeaway:** Your current processes expose you to quantifiable risk gaps in Dentistry, HVAC Service & Maintenance, and Veterinary Medicine.

Chapter I: Dental Practice Compliance (Focus Area)

Goal: Ensure seamless patient flow from inquiry $ightarrow$ booking $ightarrow validation of hygiene status.

Key Data Gaps: Insurance verification failure, missing pre-op notes, incomplete periodontal charting.

MUST-DO: Digital Intake Form

Implement a mandatory digital form capturing insurance ID and primary care physician details at the first contact point (Do not rely on phone calls).

MUST-DO: Charting Protocol

Standardize the capture of intraoral photos/diagnostics using geo-tagged metadata to prove procedural documentation integrity.

MUST-DO: Recall Workflow Automation

Automate 90-day recall scheduling based on charting data, automatically flagging any overdue patients for immediate outreach.

Chapter II: HVAC Service & Maintenance (Focus Area)

Goal: Automating service reporting and scheduling based on travel metrics and warranty triggers.

Key Data Gaps: Incomplete job site sign-off photos, failure to log specific component serial numbers, outdated weather/risk profiling.

MUST-DO: Digital Job Completion Form

Mandate the completion of a photo report *with GPS tag* for every service call, capturing meter readings and installed part tags.

MUST-DO: Predictive Maintenance Scheduling

Integrate service history with local climate data feeds to automatically suggest preventative maintenance windows before system failure is likely.

MUST-DO: Client Feedback Loop

Implement an immediate follow-up survey (within 24 hours) linked directly to the job file to capture operational satisfaction scores.

Chapter III: Veterinary Medicine Compliance (Focus Area)

Goal: Integrating EHR, inventory, and appointment reminders to ensure 100% compliance with care protocols.

Key Data Gaps: Disconnected records between pharmacy/surgery/appointment; failure to track specialized equipment usage.

MUST-DO: Integrated Records Management

Establish a single source of truth linking patient history, billing codes (CPT), and inventory consumption logs automatically.

MUST-DO: Vaccination Audit Trail

Maintain an immutable digital record for every vaccine administered, linked to the vet's license ID and date/time stamp for compliance defense.

MUST-DO: Post-Procedure Protocol Checklist

Require mandatory electronic sign-off on all post-operative care steps (e.g., bandage changes, medication dispensing) before the client leaves the facility.
