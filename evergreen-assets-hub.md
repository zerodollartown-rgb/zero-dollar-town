# Evergreen Assets Hub - Zero Dollar Town Collection

> A curated collection of high-value, non-time-sensitive guides and checklists designed to help small business owners secure their data privacy and operational autonomy.

## Included Resources

### 1. The 90-Day Operational Autonomy Guide
**File:** `blog/autonomy_guide_90_days.md`
**Topic:** A step-by-step roadmap for businesses to reduce dependency on external vendors and secure their own data stacks.
- **Why it matters:** Learn how to build internal processes that protect your business when third-party APIs change or fail.

### 2. Data Independence Checklist
**File:** `blog/data_independence_checklist.md`
**Topic:** A practical audit tool for identifying single points of failure in data management and privacy policies.
- **Why it matters:** Use this checklist to ensure your business isn't at risk from a major provider’s policy shifts or data breaches.

### 3. Industry Pain Point Audit Checklist
**File:** `blog/industry_pain_point_audit_checklist.md`
**Topic:** A comprehensive list of common failures in Dentistry, HVAC, and Veterinary sectors.
- **Why it matters:** Quickly identify if your industry has hidden risks you’re ignoring today.

### 4. Top Mistakes & Fixes: Industry Specific Guides
This section includes deep-dive analyses for specific industries:
- `blog/top_5_dentistry_data_failures.html`– The most common data integrity errors in dentistry and how to fix them.
- `blog/top_5_hvac_mistakes_data_fixes.md`– HVAC system failures that lead to expensive repairs or loss of trust.
- `blog/top_5_vet_clinic_mistakes_data_fixes.md`– Critical data gaps in veterinary clinics and their solutions.

### 5. Systemic Failure Case Studies
These files provide real-world examples of where businesses failed due to poor data management:
- `blog/dentistry_systemic_failures.html`– How systemic issues can destroy a dental practice’s reputation overnight.
- `blog/hvac_data_integrity_post.md`– Why HVAC companies are losing customers due to inconsistent service records.

### 6. Quick Reference: Missing Content Fixes
For those who need immediate help with incomplete documentation:
- `blog/dentist_missing_content.md`– A checklist for ensuring no critical patient data is left unrecorded.
- `blog/data_independence_guide.md`– Steps to regain control over your own customer information.

## How to Use This Hub
1. **Download:** Copy all files listed above into a local folder on any device (PC, tablet, or phone).
2. **Review:** Start with the 90-Day Autonomy Guide for strategic planning.
3. **Audit:** Run the Data Independence Checklist and Industry Pain Point Audit to assess current risks.
4. **Fix:** Apply fixes from the Top Mistakes guides specific to your industry (e.g., Dentistry, HVAC).
5. **Learn:** Read Systemic Failure case studies to avoid repeating others’ mistakes.
